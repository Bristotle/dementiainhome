# Consent and release

Nothing is published without a signed release. This is enforced in code: the
`Interview` and `Caregiver` types both require a `consentRef`, so a page without
one cannot be built. The reference recorded there is the identifier of the signed
document.

**This template has not been reviewed by a lawyer.** See `README.md`. Do not send
it to a clinician until it has been.

---

## Recording and publication consent

**Between:** Dementia In Home ("we", "us") and the undersigned ("you").

**Date of recording:** ______________________

**Interviewer:** ______________________

### What you are agreeing to

You agree that we may record a video interview with you, and publish that
recording, in whole or edited for length and clarity, on dementiainhome.com and
on our social media channels.

We will publish it alongside your name, your professional role, your institution
or practice, and a link to it. We will describe you accurately and we will not
alter the substance of what you say.

### What you are not agreeing to

You are **not** endorsing or recommending Dementia In Home, our caregivers, or
our service, and we will not present you as doing so. We will not use your name,
image or words in advertising, on pricing pages, or in any material that invites
someone to buy from us.

We will not imply any professional relationship between you and us beyond this
interview.

### Your control over it

- You will receive a copy of the recording for your own use, without restriction.
- You may review the page before it is published, if you ask.
- **You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, by emailing
  emmanuel@dementiacompanions.com.** We will remove the page within five working
  days of receiving your request. This right does not expire.
- If you tell us something on the recording that you would rather we cut, tell us
  and we will cut it. You do not need a reason.

### Accuracy

If we publish something that misrepresents what you said, tell us and we will
correct it promptly.

### No payment

Neither party pays the other. This is not a commercial arrangement.
