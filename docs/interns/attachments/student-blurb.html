Research and Interview Intern

Dementia In Home  ·  Remote  ·  Volunteer placement, flexible around your timetable

We are looking for two or three interns this autumn to record video interviews with dementia specialists, and publish them as a free resource for families.

What you would do

Find dementia clinicians in a given city: neurologists, geriatricians, memory clinic staff, social workers. Invite them, using our template. Record a twenty to thirty minute interview over video, from a question set we give you and send to them in advance. Then write it up: a short summary and three or four things a family could actually act on.

The interviews go on dementiainhome.com as a page about that clinician, credited to them, with your name as the interviewer.

What you get out of it

A portfolio of published interviews with named clinicians, which is difficult to build as a student. Direct contact with people practising in geriatrics, neurology and social work. Real experience of research interviewing, consent, and writing clinical material for people who are frightened and not medically trained. We will write you a reference at the end.

Hours: 5 to 10 a week, arranged around your coursework.

Compensation: this is a volunteer placement. If your programme can award credit for it, we will support that.

Location: fully remote. You need a reliable connection and somewhere quiet to record.

Equipment: we provide the recording platform and the question set.

Two honest things

You will never be asked to sell anything to anyone. And the interviews cover diagnosis, decline and end of life care, which is worth thinking about if dementia has been in your own family.

To apply

Email Emmanuel Akyeam at emmanuel@dementiacompanions.com and say why it interests you. A few sentences is plenty. A CV is welcome but not required, and there is no form.

Dementia In Home helps families find in-home caregivers for a parent living with dementia, and publishes a free guide to dementia care in each city it covers.  dementiainhome.com
