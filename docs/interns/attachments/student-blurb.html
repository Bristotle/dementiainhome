Research and Interview Intern

Dementia In Home  ·  Remote  ·  Unpaid placement, 10 hours a week, flexible around your timetable

We are looking for 5 to 7 interns this fall to record video interviews (video podcasts for YouTube) with dementia specialists, and publish them as a free resource for families. The interviews also open partnerships with those experts, which we pursue alongside the content.

What you would do

Find dementia clinicians in a given city: neurologists, geriatricians, memory clinic staff, social workers. Invite them, using our template. Record a 30 to 60 minute video interview, from a question set we give you and send to them in advance. You can also research each expert and prepare your own questions with ChatGPT, Claude or similar tools. Then prepare the interview for publication using AI tools: a short summary and 3 or 4 things a family could actually act on.

The interviews go on dementiainhome.com and dementiacompanions.com as a page about that clinician, credited to them, with your name as the interviewer.

What you get out of it

A portfolio of published interviews with named clinicians, which is difficult to build as a student. Direct contact with people practising in geriatrics, neurology and social work. Real experience of research interviewing, consent, and writing clinical material for people who are frightened and not medically trained. We will write you a reference at the end.

Hours: 10 a week, arranged around your coursework.

Compensation: this is an unpaid volunteer placement. If your program can award academic credit for it, we will support that.

Location: fully remote. You need a reliable connection and somewhere quiet to record.

Equipment: we provide the recording platform and the question set.

To apply

Email Emmanuel Akyeam at emmanuel@dementiacompanions.com and say why it interests you. A few sentences is plenty. A CV is welcome but not required, and there is no form.

Dementia In Home helps families find in-home caregivers for a parent living with dementia, and publishes a free guide to dementia care in each of the 20 cities it covers.  dementiainhome.com
