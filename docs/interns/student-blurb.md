# Student blurb

What a programme forwards to its students. Sara Robinson at USC asked for "a
ready-to-send email or flyer", and Cynthia Hancock at UNC Charlotte offered to
pass something on to her gerontology students, both within a day of the first
send. This is that.

It is deliberately not `placement-description.md`. That one answers a placements
officer's questions about the arrangement. This one is written for a student
deciding in about twenty seconds whether to read on, so it opens with what they
would do rather than who we are.

**Two blanks must be filled before this is sent.** See `README.md`. Both are the
first thing a student asks and neither is mine to invent.

---

**Subject: Remote internship: record video interviews with dementia specialists**

Hello,

We are looking for two or three interns this autumn to record video interviews
with dementia specialists, and publish them as a free resource for families.

**What you would do.** Find dementia clinicians in a given city, neurologists,
geriatricians, memory clinic staff, social workers. Invite them, using our
template. Record a twenty to thirty minute interview over video, from a question
set we give you and send them in advance. Then write it up: a short summary and
three or four things a family could actually act on.

The interviews go on dementiainhome.com as a page about that clinician, credited
to them, with your name as the interviewer.

**What you get out of it.** A portfolio of published interviews with named
clinicians, which is difficult to build as a student. Direct contact with people
practising in geriatrics, neurology and social work. Real experience of research
interviewing, consent and writing clinical material for people who are frightened
and not medically trained. We will write you a reference at the end.

**The practical part.** Remote, [HOURS] hours a week, flexible around your
timetable. [COMPENSATION]. We provide the recording platform and the questions.
You need a reliable connection and somewhere quiet.

**Two honest things.** You will never be asked to sell anything to anyone. And
the interviews cover diagnosis, decline and end of life care, which is worth
thinking about if dementia has been in your own family.

**To apply**, email Emmanuel Akyeam at emmanuel@dementiacompanions.com and say
why it interests you. A few sentences is plenty. A CV is welcome but not
required, and there is no form.

Dementia In Home helps families find in-home caregivers for a parent living with
dementia, and publishes a free guide to dementia care in each city it covers.

---

## Notes for whoever sends this

Keep the two honest things in. The first is what separates this from the sales
internships students are used to being offered, and the second is a real duty of
care given the subject.

Do not add a deadline unless there is one. A false deadline on a placement a
student is weighing against coursework reads as pressure and costs more
applications than it gains.

If a programme asks for a flyer rather than an email, this text fits on one page
with the headings as they are.
